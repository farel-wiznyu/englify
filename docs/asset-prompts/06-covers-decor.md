# Category covers and decor sticker pack

> Run in ONE chat per sub-group. Covers (cover-*): start a new chat, paste the STYLE BIBLE, attach the Ollie reference image, then paste the 8 cover prompts one at a time.
> Decor (decor-*): start a new chat, paste the STYLE BIBLE only (no attachment), then paste the 10 decor prompts one at a time.
> Re-paste the STYLE BIBLE after every 6-8 images, or as soon as the style starts drifting (outlines thinner, colors muddy).

## cover-colors.png - Category card cover for "Colors" (OPTIONAL, category grid)
- Canvas: 1024x1024, transparent background
- Attach: Ollie reference
```text
Same style bible. Same Ollie as the attached reference image. Create a category cover for "Colors": a lively cluster of three fat, glossy paint blobs (one red, one sky blue, one sunny yellow) plus one chunky paintbrush with an orange handle. The blobs are caught mid-splat, squashed and stretched like rubbery jelly, each blob with a tiny glossy white glint and small flying paint droplets around them. The brush is mid-swoosh, leaving a thick curly stroke of hot pink behind it. Ollie peeks in from the bottom-right corner, his head and one stubby wing visible, leaning in off-balance with his crooked glasses sliding down his beak, huge wide eyes and a cheeky open-beak grin. Funny gag: Ollie has a big splotch of blue paint on his nose and one ear tuft is dripping yellow, and he has no idea. Add three or four small sparkles. ```
```
- Tip: If the blobs come out looking like ice cream or poop, ask for "flat paint splats with a thick wavy puddle edge, clearly liquid paint".
